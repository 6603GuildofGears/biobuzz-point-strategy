What should we focus on?

A HIVE TIP is 20 points. An owned FLOWER element is 2. Missed shots and slow intakes waste cycle time — that usually matters more than perfect field physics. NECTAR dunks are harder (3.6 in ball, 4 in hole). Flower ownership is “last nectar of your color on top”; you can still steal a flower by dunking yours, but you cannot pick up theirs.
